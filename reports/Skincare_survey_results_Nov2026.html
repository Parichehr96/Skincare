Budgets are steady: 32 of 55 usually spend €10–20 on one product. 9 spend under €10, and 14 spend €20 or more.

How you picked the product you use now

Online reviews are named most. Social media and friends together come up even more often, so word of mouth still counts. 49 answered, up to two picks each.

Shelves reach beyond the drugstore: 17 of 49 named a brand outside the usual drugstore names, and 10 use Korean or Japanese skincare. Among drugstore brands, La Roche-Posay was named most (9), then CeraVe (5).

How fast you know if it works

34 of 49

can tell within a week: 18 after a few days and 16 after about a week.

15 of 49

need longer: 11 about two to three weeks, and 4 a month or more.

A first impression is often about how a product feels, like texture, shine or stinging. Changes such as fewer breakouts or a more even tone take longer to show.

Would you trust an AI chat to suggest a product?

Few would trust it on its own. A third would, if it shows reviews from real women. 54 answered.

The questions people wrote down show what would help: how a product was tested, who checked it, and whether anyone is paid to recommend it.

A big star rating, or reviews from women like you?

Asked to choose between "4.3 stars from 2,300 reviews" and "11 of 14 women with skin like yours would buy it again", most picked the big rating. Younger women were split almost evenly.

How many reviews from women with similar skin before you'd trust a product? 10 was enough for 23 of the 43 who named a number, and 20 was enough for all of them. 12 said the number doesn't matter.

Sharing a review to read everyone's reviews

What you'd ask

24 people wrote a question they'd ask about skincare. Most are about two things: is this right for my skin (10), and what's in it (7).

"Waarom juist die voor mij goed zou werken?"
"Have any been flagged as acne causing or pore clogging?"
"Van welke zonnebrand krijg ik geen puistjes?"
"How was it tested, who approved it?"

Survey run by Parichehr Talebzadeh, 27 October to 7 November 2026. 55 women in the Netherlands took part. Not everyone answered every question, so totals differ per question.
